## Payments

### Basic License

Basic CMS payments are paid to us, which go toward paying for the hosting and buying a paid shared license for the basic CMS.

### Pro License

Pro CMS payments are paid directly to us, and then we forward the required amount to Agentejo. Pro CMS license payments are strictly for the CMS license and hosting costs. Roughly 89% of the $300 goes to Agentejo for the license ([check updated conversion](https://www.google.com/search?q=250+EUR+to+USD)), the remaining goes to hosting costs and fees we pay to Stripe / PayPal and Agentejo.

#### Discounts

Agentejo offers a 50% discount on the Pro license only for verified non-profits, open source projects or educational institutions.
