## Sign up

A DangoID Account allows you to login to all of our services. To get started, head to [my.dangoweb.com](https://my.dangoweb.com). Click "Register", and fill in all fields. Complete a Captcha if required, and then click "Register" at the bottom. If preferred instead, you may create your account using an external account provider listed on the page. You now have a DangoID Account!

## Log in

Logging into the DangoID Dashboard is straightforward. On [my.dangoweb.com](https://my.dangoweb.com), fill in your username/email and password.

## Edit account

If you would like to edit your DangoID Account details, log in to MyDangoID and enter your new desired details.
