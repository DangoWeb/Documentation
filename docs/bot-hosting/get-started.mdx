## Variables

Once you have created and paid for your bot hosting, you will receive an email with instructions on how to access the control panel. From there, log in and select your server. You will be able to manage your bot's variables, such as Git Repo.

## Limits

Hosting bots requires a good bit of disk space and memory. Check with your current provider to check your bot size. CPU usage is not an issue.

Type|Disk Space|Memory|CPU|I/O|Swap
-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----
Paid ⚡|10GB|2GB|100%|500|4GB

High limits marked with ⚡.

If you have a lot of unused files or packages taking up space, we can get rid of them for you on request.
