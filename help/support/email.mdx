To reach us via email, please send your inquiries to [admin@dangoweb.com](mailto:admin@dangoweb.com). We aim to respond to all email inquiries within 48 hours. Please provide as much detail as possible about your issue to help us assist you effectively.

If your issue is related to account access, please include your username and any error messages you are encountering. If possible, send the email from the address associated with your account for verification purposes.

If your issue is related to billing, please include your account information and any relevant transaction details in your email.

For faster assistance, we recommend using a [support ticket](/help/support/ticket).

If your matter is urgent, [text or call us](/help/support/phone) (US-based, carrier fees may apply).
