We understand the struggle that many students face when searching for adequate hosting for their class or hobby projects. Most hosting providers set extremely low limits on free plans and enforce pricing which may not be accessible for zero-income underaged students.

Dango Web Solutions offers Web Hosting for verified students attending primary school, secondary school, university, or homeschooling through a completely free plan.

All users have the option to bring their own subdomain or domain to use for hosting, as long as they own the required DNS records. For students that cannot afford a domain, we offer free subdomains for verified users, strictly following our legal policies.

Students must be identified through ID card, full name, email address, location, or school affiliation to verify student status. The full name on the account must match the ID card and/or email address. Email addresses using a `.edu` domain are automatically labeled as education accounts and can be used to access our free educational resources. We do not retain unnessary user data for this process. All data used to verify student account status, such as ID card, full name, location, and school affiliation will be promptly removed from our systems after verification of the student. Users may request deletion of their account, services, and all related data at any time.

Users must be 13 or older to sign up for a DangoID account. Students under 13 are required to have consent from a parent or guardian to create an account.

When activating a free Web Hosting plan for education, users will be prompted to explain their desired use of the service, and must follow our legal policies.

Educational organizations, schools, and classrooms may register students for Web Hosting through Reseller Web Hosting. A DangoID account and student verification are not necessary to utilize Reseller Web Hosting as a user. Please contact your educational organization for more information on how to log in.
