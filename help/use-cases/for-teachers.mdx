Teachers may want bulk web hosting user accounts for students in their classrooms for class projects or showcases. Dango Web Solutions provides Reseller Web Hosting for education at a reduced, affordable cost. We offer Unlimited and Limited plans that are based on desired subuser resources.

Resellers have the ability to provision subdomains of an added domain to subusers, giving students their own home for their files and classwork. Given a main domain of `teacher.com`, subdomains such as `student.teacher.com` and `student2.teacher.com` can be provisioned to students manually or automatically via scripting.

Please note that all users (including subusers) are expected to be 13 years of age or older to utilize our hosting due to consent to data collection. Students under 13 are required to have consent from a parent or guardian to use hosting, and teachers are expected to track and follow this requirement.

A DangoID account and student verification are not necessary to utilize Reseller Web Hosting as a user.
