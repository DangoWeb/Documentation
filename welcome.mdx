## Websites


<Steps>
  <Step title="Domain">
    Register a [domain](/domains) for your website. This is where your visitors will see your website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Website Creation">
    Create a website using [code](/quickstart), one of our many supported [Website Builders](/website-creation/website-builders), or request a [custom commission](/website-creation/commissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Web Hosting">
    Host your website on web hosting and connect it to your domain.
  </Step>
</Steps>
